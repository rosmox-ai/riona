# Content 09 · Careers — Conditional Material

A small careers page or Contact route is optional if the company is recruiting. Do not imply a large workforce, established training programme, benefits package or internal promotion record.

- Heading: Do work that matters, every day.
- Lead: Interested in being part of Riona's journey? Explore current opportunities to work with us.

Publish only real openings with approved role, location, requirements, application method and owner. If none exist, say “There are no advertised openings at present” only after confirmation. Offer speculative applications only if the company will handle them and has an appropriate privacy process.

No fictional jobs, employee quotes, staff counts or recruitment email domain. No CV uploads in the design preview. Keep a future job template simple: role → responsibilities → requirements → verified application route.
