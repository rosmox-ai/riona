# Content 12 · Legal and Accessibility Outlines

Outlines for preparation, not publishable legal policies. Use the actual legal entity, jurisdictions, services and contact channels. Do not invent an officer, CIN, domain or office.

## Privacy policy

Identify the organisation and contact; actual data collected; purposes and applicable basis; service providers; storage/access; retention; rights and requests; transfers where relevant; children where applicable; changes and effective date. Separate business enquiries, recruitment and safety processing where used. No blanket promise of deletion when another obligation may apply.

## Terms of use

Scope of website use, ownership, permitted use, information limitations, third-party resources, applicable law and contact. Obtain appropriate approval before publication.

## Cookies — conditional

Document actual tracking and storage. Add consent controls and a cookie policy where applicable; do not describe unused tools or place a decorative banner on a site without defining its behaviour.

## Medical/product notice — conditional

Use approved wording appropriate to the actual product information and intended audience. A disclaimer cannot substitute for accurate, authorised content.

## Grievance or privacy contact

Publish verified roles and channels according to the company's applicable obligations. No fictional officer or unmonitored address.

## Accessibility

Describe the accessibility work actually completed, known limitations, review date and a verified support contact. Do not claim conformance solely from the design brief. Draft intent: “We want everyone to be able to use this website. If you experience difficulty, please contact us through the published contact channel.”

## Downloads

Publish policies and reports only when genuine approved files exist. All preview placeholders must be removed before launch.
