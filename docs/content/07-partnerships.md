# Content 07 · Partnerships

## PART-01 · Hero

- Eyebrow: PARTNER WITH RIONA
- Headline: Let's build what's next in healthcare, together.
- Lead: Good partnerships begin with a clear conversation. Tell us about your needs and explore how we could work together.
- Action: Start a conversation

## PART-02 · Opportunities — confirm before display

Use only the actual collaboration types Riona offers. Contract manufacturing, licensing and global distribution are not assumed.

Card template: `[confirmed collaboration type]` → `[who it is for]` → `[what Riona actually provides]` → Discuss your requirements.

## PART-03 · How we begin

- Share your needs: Tell us about your organisation and what you are looking for.
- Explore the fit: Discuss the scope, expectations and questions that matter.
- Agree next steps: Establish a clear path forward together.

These are proposed enquiry steps, not claims of existing procedures. No guaranteed turnaround, NDA promise, technology-transfer timeline or commercial outcome.

## PART-04 · FAQ — optional

Include real answers to the questions the company can address. Useful prompts: What enquiries do you welcome? What information should I include? Who will follow up? Omit unanswered questions rather than inventing capabilities or service promises.

## PART-05 · Enquiry

- Heading: Let's talk.
- Text: Share a few details about your requirements.
- Action: Contact Riona

Use the short Contact form, or the same component inline if useful. No multi-step funnel, fabricated testimonial, numerical proof strip or gated brochure. A capability document can be linked directly once supplied.
