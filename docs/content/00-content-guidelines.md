# Content 00 · Voice, Facts and Demo Rules

Demo copy supports layout review. Riona is newly established. Do not describe it as a large, established manufacturer or replace removed figures with smaller invented ones.

## Voice

Precise, calm, warm and clear. Keep useful wording from the original draft where it does not imply unverified facts. Use Indian English and specific action labels. Avoid boastful claims, excessive technical jargon and repetitive decorative italics.

Distinguish commitments from evidence: “We value clear communication” is proposed brand language; “98% on-time dispatch” is an operating claim and must not be invented. Terms such as manufacturer, CDMO, research centre and vertically integrated require business confirmation.

## Demo handling

Use a visible “Design preview — demo wording; company details pending confirmation” label on every demo preview, regardless of deployment mode. It must be visible on mobile as well. Preview forms must not collect or submit data. Prevent public indexing and protect previews where possible.

Editorial placeholders such as `[approved contact email]` are instructions, not publishable text. Do not use fictional medicine strengths, approvals, customer quotations, addresses, employee quotes or realistic news announcements. Use optional-section notes rather than filling unsupported sections.

Record image sources and usage rights. Stock images must be described as illustrative, never as Riona's own facilities. The same fact rules apply to alt text, metadata, download names, structured data and social previews.

## Launch review

- Confirm business description and every factual claim.
- Replace or remove all editorial placeholders and demo material.
- Obtain approved product information and legal text where applicable.
- Verify contact details and operational responsibility for each active form.
- Check accessible labels, truthful status messages and image descriptions.
- Publish only real vacancies, reports, downloads and company updates.
- Remove the preview label only after demo content is resolved and publication is approved.
