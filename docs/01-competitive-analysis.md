# 01 · Competitive Analysis: Reference Websites

Revised 4 November 2026 for a newly established Riona Life Science. The goal is to learn from the references without copying their scale, business models or feature lists.

## 1. Evidence and limitations

The original research described text extraction, browser captures at approximately tablet width and measured CSS. Supporting captures, CSS extracts and performance reports were not included in the supplied documents. Its precise visual measurements and rankings should therefore be treated as provisional rather than independently established facts.

During the subsequent review, accessible homepage content supported several structural observations for Biocon, Bioneeds and Charles River. Vimta timed out and Eurofins blocked automated access. Those access failures describe the review environment, not the experience of ordinary visitors.

No conversion analytics, comprehensive accessibility audit, mobile usability study or field performance measurements were supplied. Do not infer user behaviour, image provenance or legal compliance from appearance or an automated page fetch.

## 3. Business-model differences

These are references for communication and design, not a uniform competitor set. Biocon's product/corporate emphasis differs from research and testing businesses. A safety-reporting feature or investor section is not universally required simply because one reference includes it.

Riona's actual model remains to be confirmed. Do not translate reference-site facilities, therapies, accreditation lists or customer journeys into claims about Riona.

## 4. Design interpretation

The following are recommendations, not measured findings:

- A predominantly white canvas is a suitable direction for the requested professional tone.
- Clear introductory language should identify the business and customer once those facts are supplied.
- One focused hero is a simpler starting point than rotating messages. There is no supplied evidence for a specific carousel conversion penalty.
- Readable typography, strong image selection and consistent alignment matter more than feature quantity.
- Relevant evidence is useful when real. A new company can establish clarity through its actual people, offering and working approach without fabricated metrics.
- Enquiry access should be easy. A sticky bar, multi-step form or gated document is not automatically better than a visible link and short form.
- Editorial stories can explain genuine work. Do not fabricate case studies to fill a layout.

## 5. Adopt, adapt and leave out

| Adopt | Adapt to actual needs | Leave out of the initial Riona plan |
|---|---|---|
| Clear labels and readable content | Customer-journey structure after confirming the business model | Large-scale statistics and international maps |
| Carefully composed photography | Service/product browsing after approved offerings exist | Invented credentials, testimonials and company histories |
| Accessible navigation and forms | Safety information when applicable and operational | Mega menus for a small sitemap |
| Direct enquiry access | Actual company stories when available | Pinned scroll sequences and decorative count-ups |
| Restraint in typography and colour | Video only with a relevant asset and usable fallback | A newsroom without real news |

## 6. What “better” means for this project

Assess Riona against its visitors' tasks rather than assigning unsupported star ratings to other businesses:

1. Can a visitor explain what Riona does after reading the introduction?
2. Can they identify the relevant offering and enquiry route without unnecessary navigation?
3. Are company claims accurate and clearly distinguished from aspirations?
4. Does the layout remain readable and usable on mobile, with keyboard navigation and zoom?
5. Are imagery, spacing and typography consistent across the homepage and interior pages?
6. Does the page remain useful when motion is disabled or nonessential scripts fail?
7. Are downloads, contacts and form status messages truthful and operational?
