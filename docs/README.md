# Riona Life Science: Website Planning Docs

Research, visual direction, page structure and demo copy for a new pharmaceutical company. Planning only; no website implementation has started.

## Current direction — 4 November 2026

Riona is a newly established company. The founder has explicitly rejected the earlier invented scale, operating history and statistics. Do not replace those figures with smaller invented figures. Team size is unconfirmed and should not appear on the website.

The site should feel professional through clarity, typography, photography and careful execution. Keep the useful existing language, but remove statements implying an established manufacturing network, approvals, customer results or international footprint.

## Decisions

- Light theme: white, quiet off-white surfaces, ink text and a restrained dark footer.
- Proposed cobalt primary and small teal accent, subject to the eventual logo.
- Two families only: Manrope for headings; Inter for body and UI. No serif accent or mono family.
- One focused hero. A strong still image is the default; video is optional.
- Native scrolling, restrained transitions, no count-up statistics or pinned scroll sequence.
- No company statistics, founding timeline, global map, accreditation strips or invented testimonials.
- A compact corporate website with one straightforward enquiry journey.
- Product catalogue, manufacturing pages, science pages, safety intake and news are conditional on real business needs and approved material.

## Confirmed and unresolved

Confirmed: brand name supplied as **Riona Life Science**; a new pharma company; professional light-theme direction; demo wording for design review; eventual Next.js implementation.

Still needed: legal name and domain, exact business model, actual offerings, primary customer, location, brand assets, approved imagery, contact details and form owner. Owned manufacturing or research facilities must not be inferred from the company category.
